How the PASS filter works
  • Each player must have at least 10 prior recorded matches.
  • The enhanced model must beat the Elo baseline on chronological holdout Brier score.
  • The selected side must have at least 65% model probability.
  • More than 120 days without a recorded match triggers a context warning, not an automatic PASS.

The Monte Carlo interval measures simulation noise only, not model uncertainty. This is research output, not a guarantee or a live betting recommendation.
